problems, but must not write, rewrite, or suggest replacement prose. This includes
interface text, prompts, and documentation. Implementing a supplied string does
not authorize changing its wording.

Use American English for authored interface text, including "catalog".
Preserve the spelling in source quotations, submitted content, and proper names.
