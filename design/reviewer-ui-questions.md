# Reviewer interaction pass

Controlling feedback: Maz's September 6 review of table sorting, filtering,
section navigation, RICE entry and administrator request notes. Desktop is the
design target. The current queue, request, RICE and administrator screens were
captured at 1440 × 1000 before inspecting their implementation.

## Tables

1. Which column determines the order, and in which direction?
2. How can I change that order or filter without losing my place?
3. Are the displayed results current, updating, empty or unavailable?

Keep the existing table and filter controls mounted while results update. Use
plain-text column buttons with the direction arrow on the active sort. Keep
keyboard focus visible on controls without moving the document or table.

## Request review

1. Which part of this request am I reviewing?
2. What information and decision belong to this section?
3. Has an administrator left information I need to consider?

Retain the section menu and current selection. Selecting a section must not
scroll or focus its entire content. Show administrator notes with their author
and date, separate from requester statements and model suggestions.

## RICE reach

1. What am I counting, and can I name a unit other than the examples?
2. How many units benefit during the period I specify?
3. Where does the estimate come from?

State that the counting unit is open text. Accept a whole-number count without
spinner controls; retain the unit, period and source alongside that number.

## Administrator

1. Which requests need attention, who is responsible next, and what happens next?
2. What note should the reviewer see on this request?
3. Was my note recorded, and will it survive leaving the page?

Use representative reference requests with truthful ownership and varied
statuses. Add a persistent note through the request's existing workflow and
show the same saved note to reviewers. Do not alter visitor work to improve
the examples.
