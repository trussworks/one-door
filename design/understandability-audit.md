## Evidence

- [Reader questions](understandability-questions.md) record the tasks before source changes.
- `screenshots/understandability/01-*-1440.png` are as-found captures; `02-stripped-*-1440.png` record the stripped compositions.
- `08-*-1440.png`, `08-*-1280.png` and `09-*-text-200.png` are production captures of intake, queue, request, matches, risk, completion, catalog and reports. They contain no development overlay. The earlier `06`/`07` captures are retained as intermediate evidence.
- `10-rice-step-*-1440.png` show all four RICE steps after saving has settled. The four-step flow was also exercised in Chrome, including native required validation, draft recovery and saved score; `04` captures are interim states.
- Browser acceptance verifies the requester-to-reviewer journey, current evidence, rejection reasons, queue/caption/sort context, RICE, handoff and fulfillment. Independent Chrome review covered the major changed surfaces at desktop widths and enlarged text.
- The final captures include the requester/agency display order matching its heading sort and completion prerequisites grouped into links to unfinished review sections. A subsequent small copy correction labels the queue count as “Information gaps: 1”.
- Rules unchanged by this pass retain their evidence from [the preceding screen review](workflows-four-audit.md), checked against the unchanged styling and the new compositions. Those entries are not claims of a new measurement.

## Limits

The model's core suggestion decisions passed the three-case follow-up, but individual reviewer-facing explanations can still be wrong or unnecessary; [the exact outputs](intake-timing-20260906.json) retain those limitations. Empty and failure states are covered by browser/integration tests, not by every listed screenshot. Actual screen-reader speech and localization were not tested. A separate mobile design remains outside the agreed scope.

The catalog comparison still shows source values without automatically highlighting conflicting cells. Seeded risk explanations remain illustrative. Neither limitation was changed in this pass.
