# Pass two: task-first revision

Maz's September 5 walkthrough controls this pass. The working application remains the deliverable; the changes use its saved records and domain actions.

## Landing

What does One Door help with? Which of the three paths is mine? Where does that path take me? Can I change views later?

## Waiting for assistance

Did my action work? Is the request waiting or being processed? What am I waiting to receive? Can I stay here, or safely leave and return? What can I do if the call fails?

## Review and submit

Did One Door understand my need? What might help, and what would remain unresolved? Can I leave that judgment to OIT? What exactly will I send, and what happens after submission?

Keep the saved request visible and editable. Group its facts into readable sections. Distinguish optional opinions about existing assets from the service-routing choice and required task-end rating. Do not make a requester read internal evidence to find the next action.

## Request queues

Which requests need my work? Which unassigned requests need someone to take responsibility? For an administrator, who owns each request and where is work waiting? Can I open a record and return to the same queue?

Reviewers get their assignments and work without a coordinator. Administrators get an explicit all-requests queue and person filters. This is navigation within a freely switchable demo, not new role-based authorization.

## Shared controls

What does each filter change? Which filters are active? Do labels and controls align consistently? Is the empty state one useful answer with a way forward?

## Evidence and review

Captures use a separate browser-test database and controlled provider. No test invokes Anthropic or alters Maz's requests. Preserve before, stripped, and rebuilt captures. Agent verification is not human design approval; Maz/design's next review remains pending.
