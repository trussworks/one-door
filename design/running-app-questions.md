# Running app: tasks guiding integration

These carry forward the approved questions from the revised experience artifact. Screenshots 20–23 capture the actual React application, not the standalone prototype. The human design gate applies to release; implementation continues while it is pending.

## Requester

What can One Door help me accomplish? Where is my saved work? What does OIT still need from me? Which suggested service fits, what is still missing, and what exactly am I submitting?

## Reviewer

Which request needs my next decision? What did the requester say? Which recommendations and evidence apply to the current revision? Who owns each remaining task? What must be settled before handoff, and what is required before fulfillment?

## Catalog steward

Which inventory items are approved, disputed, overdue, or unpublished? What do the different sources actually say? What values should we publish and why? Can I preserve unfinished reconciliation, and can the next steward see the original evidence and earlier decisions?

## Administrator

Where is work waiting and who can move it? Are external statuses current? Which completed outcomes and survey responses support each metric? Can I open exactly the records included in a number?

The composition follows these tasks: requester home and one workspace; reviewer queue and one record with an attached RICE panel; administrator Dashboard, Catalog, and Reports. Technical states do not each get their own page.
