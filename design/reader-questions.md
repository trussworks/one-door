# Reader questions

Written after capturing the empty repository at 1440 x 900 and before creating any screen source.

## Submitter flow

1. What can I ask OIT for here?
2. What problem and outcome does OIT need me to describe?
3. What did the system understand, and how do I correct it before submitting?
4. Does OIT already provide something that could solve the problem?
5. What requirements or risks affect the request?
6. What happens next, and can I leave and resume without losing work?
7. Where is the request now?

## Reviewer flow

1. Which request needs attention next?
2. What does the requester need, in their own words and as structured requirements?
3. Why did the system recommend each existing solution, risk finding, and priority factor?
4. What can I accept or override, and what does the decision change?
5. Who owns the request, how long has it been in the current stage, and what is blocking it?
6. What changed, who changed it, and can I reconstruct the decision later?

## Satisfaction report

1. How many submitters completed the intake?
2. Did at least 90% report a satisfactory experience?
3. Where did submitters struggle or abandon the flow?
