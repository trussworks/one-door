# Requester intake: one evolving request

Maz approved this flow after reviewing live model output on September 5, 2026. It supersedes the requester's repeated confirmation pages, separate asset-discovery wait, and service/asset selection lists. The five supplied screenshots are the problem statement; previous design-pass checks do not certify this new flow.

## The reader's questions, in task order

1. What do I need to tell OIT about the work I want to accomplish?
2. Which missing facts can I answer, and is it okay not to know?
3. What did the assistance add or change in my request?
4. Is there one clear existing way to meet my need, and can I leave that judgment to OIT?
5. What am I sending, how do I finish, and how do I return later?

## Design constraints

- One workspace and one concise evolving request record; no repeated long summaries or technical candidate lists.
- At most two requester-intake calls, started explicitly. Later edits stay editable without launching another call. Reviewer preparation happens after submission.
- Questions ask for business facts, observable success, and constraints—not decisions about OIT's internal architecture.
- Silence or uncertainty does not block submission. A failed model call leaves a usable saved request and a human review path.
- One current, credible strong-fit suggestion at most; component-level or uncertain matches belong to the reviewer. Fit confirmation is not approval, routing, access, or fulfillment.
- Original words, unknowns, edits, and evidence remain available to OIT. Required task-end ratings and human-only RICE stay unchanged.
- Save status names completed persistence, not an attempted write. Input/action groups need consistent spacing.
